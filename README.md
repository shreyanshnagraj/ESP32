Assignment 
I have worked on the level 1 of the electronic and embedded assignment where we have to work on the ESP32 , I used the ultrasonic sensor to detect the distance and assign how the servo motor worked, here i used single horn servo because it will help in easy movement of the tail . Attempted to implement the bonus push button feature but failed. Tried to implement it ( Used little  bit AI ) but got confused in between . According to the assignment implemented woking according to the distance i.e.
•	Above 50 cm — slow tail movement.
•	10–50 cm — fast tail movement.
•	Below 10 cm — tail stops and red LED turns on.
 Got little help from the earlier experience with Arduino .
